About Glute Base Gym

Built by fitness enthusiasts who believe in real iron, scientific biomechanics, and zero gimmicks.

Our Story

Why We Created Glute Base

Most local gyms in Bengaluru are equipped with the same generic setup: a couple of loose dumbbells, rickety benches, and one crowded cable station. We founded Glute Base Gym & Fitness Centre in Venkateshwara Layout to change that standard.

We recognized that modern lifters—especially students, working professionals, and athletes—want targeted training tools: heavy Olympic barbells, plate-loaded leg presses, smooth dual cable crossovers, and specialized posterior chain equipment designed for glute hypertrophy and lower back resilience.

Located in the vibrant student community of SG Palya, just minutes away from Christ University Central Campus, we provide a high-energy, clean, and motivating space where every member is supported to reach their true physical potential.

Training Philosophy

The Four Pillars of Glute Base

How our training approach delivers real physical transformation without shortcuts.

📈

Progressive Overload

We track weights, reps, and sets. Your muscles adapt when challenged systematically over time with measurable volume progression.

📐

Biomechanical Form

Ego lifting leads to joint injuries. Our floor trainers emphasize joint alignment, full range of motion, and mind-muscle contraction on every rep.

🥗

Practical Nutrition

No extreme starvation diets or snake-oil fat burners. We guide you on protein intake, calorie balances, and affordable whole foods suited for hostel or home living.

Convenient Location

How to Reach Glute Base Gym

Located in Venkateshwara Layout, SG Palya with easy pedestrian and two-wheeler access.

🎓

From Christ University

Exit through the Central Campus Back Gate onto SG Palya Main Road. Walk 350 meters down to 4th Cross Road. Take a right past Gayatri Gents PG. Gym is 100 meters ahead on your left. 5 mins walking time.

🏍️

From Tavarekere Main Road

Turn into Venkateshwara Layout via 1st Main / Srinivasa Theatre road. Connect to 4th Cross. Ample shaded two-wheeler parking available directly in front of the gym entrance. 2 mins by bike.

🚗

From Dairy Circle / Koramangala

Take Hosur Road towards SG Palya signal, turn into SG Palya Main Road towards 4th Cross. Cabs and autos drop off right at the gym entrance. 5-7 mins transit time.

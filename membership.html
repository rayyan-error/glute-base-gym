Frequently Asked Questions

Membership Questions Answered

Everything you need to know before joining Glute Base Gym.

1. Are there any hidden registration or admission fees?

No. Unlike commercial franchises that charge ₹1,500 to ₹3,000 as "joining fees", our published prices are all-inclusive. You get immediate access to all equipment without any additional admission charges.

2. Can I freeze my membership if I go home for college vacations or exams?

Yes! We accommodate college breaks and semester holidays. Annual members receive a 30-day free pause window, and 6-month members can pause for up to 15 days upon prior notice at the reception counter.

3. Can I take a free trial before paying for a membership?

Yes! We offer a 1-day complimentary workout pass so you can inspect the machines, test the dumbbell range, and experience the gym environment first-hand. Claim your free trial pass here →

4. What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all UPI applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), cash, and direct bank transfers at the reception counter. Instant digital payment receipt will be issued on your WhatsApp number.

5. What are the operating timings of the gym?

We are open Monday through Saturday from 6:00 AM to 10:30 PM with continuous all-day access. On Sundays, we are open in the evening from 3:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
